# Next three community placements

All three are **nofollow**. They do not pass ranking equity. What they do pass is
traffic, and on Reddit and Hacker News the thread itself often ranks in Google —
so your link sits inside a page that outranks your own.

Work them in this order. One per day, not all at once.

---

## 1. Hacker News — Show HN (today, 10 minutes)

Biggest upside, lowest effort, and it either works in two hours or not at all.

**Where:** news.ycombinator.com → submit
**When:** 7:30–9:00 PM IST on a weekday (that is morning US Eastern, when the
front page turns over).

**Title** — must start with "Show HN:" and must not sell.

```
Show HN: Hosting cost calculator that shows the renewal price, not the intro price
```

**URL**

```
https://mohitkoli.in/tools/hosting-cost-calculator
```

**Text field:** leave it empty. Put what you have to say in a comment instead —
HN treats a filled text field on a Show HN as marketing.

**First comment, post it immediately after submitting:**

```
I build small sites for clients in India and kept redoing the same arithmetic, so I built the thing that does it.

Hosting is advertised at one rate and renewed at another. The gap is larger than most people expect: the shared tier I use goes from ₹149/mo on a four-year term to ₹449/mo at renewal. The advertised per-month figure is also a division, not a billing cycle — you pay the whole term in one invoice, and 18% GST is added at checkout, which dollar-priced comparisons never show.

Pick a plan, pick how many years you will realistically keep the site, and it shows both numbers side by side plus the real invoice. No signup, runs entirely client side, static page.

Open about it: the buy button is a referral link, which is what pays for the site. Everything above it is the provider's own published pricing, including the renewal rate they would rather you compared after you bought.

The arithmetic generalises — I would happily add other providers if anyone wants to check my numbers against theirs.
```

**Expect this to be a coin flip.** HN skews US and the pricing is Indian, so it
may get no traction at all. It costs ten minutes and the downside is zero.

**Rules that will get you banned:** never ask anyone to upvote, never post the
link anywhere asking for support, and reply to critical comments properly rather
than defensively. HN detects vote rings and the penalty is permanent.

## 2. Reddit (only if the two-week warmup actually happened)

If you created the account around 16 October and have been answering questions
without links since, the window is open. **If you did not, do not post — start
the warmup today instead and come back in two weeks.** A first post with a link
on a new account is removed and the account is finished.

Order matters: start in the smaller subreddits, not r/developersIndia.

**r/SideProject** — no warmup culture, friendly to tools.

```
Title: I built a calculator because hosting renewal pricing is deliberately hard to compare

Body: same content as the Hacker News comment above, minus the referral line
(put that in a reply if someone asks — on Reddit an unprompted disclosure in the
post body reads as an ad).
```

**r/webdev** — read their self-promotion rule first; they have a dedicated
showcase thread and posting outside it gets removed.

**r/developersIndia** — the one that matters. Your exact audience. Do not open
with a link. Post the numbers as a text post with no URL at all, and put the
calculator in a comment only if someone asks for it. That subreddit removes
self-promotion fast and the moderators are strict.

```
Title: Hosting renewal pricing in India — I tabulated the actual gap across four tiers

Body: the four-tier table. No link. Answer questions in the comments.
```

## 3. Facebook groups (most underrated for India)

Almost nobody doing SEO bothers with these, and they are where a large part of
the Indian WordPress and small-business web crowd actually is. Zero SEO value,
real traffic.

Search Facebook for and join:

```
WordPress India
Web Designers and Developers India
Digital Marketing India
Freelancers India
Indian Startups
```

**Do not post on day one.** Join, then answer two or three hosting questions
over a week with the numbers and no link. After that, a post like the Indie
Hackers one is accepted rather than removed.

Groups vary enormously — some ban links outright, some do not care. Read the
pinned rules before your first post in each.

---

## What to skip, and why

- **Stack Exchange / Server Fault** — hosting recommendation questions are
  closed as off-topic there, and a link in an answer gets it deleted.
- **Warrior Forum, BlackHatWorld, any "link exchange" group** — the links are
  worthless and the company you keep is a signal of its own.
- **Medium again** — you already posted there. A second post this soon splits
  your own audience rather than adding one.

---

## The honest expectation

None of this moves a Google ranking by itself. Three good threads might send a
few hundred visitors and leave three pages that mention your domain. The ranking
lever is still referring domains, measured on the first of each month.

Where these do help: a Reddit or HN thread that ranks for "hosting cost india"
puts your link on a page with authority you will not have for years.
