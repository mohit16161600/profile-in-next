## Verified figures — isse bahar koi number nahi

Hostinger India, 17 September 2026 ko check kiya:

```
KVM 1    ₹599/mo   →  ₹999/mo     +67%
KVM 2    ₹799/mo   →  ₹1,199/mo   +50%
KVM 4    ₹1,099/mo →  ₹2,399/mo   +118%
KVM 8    ₹2,199/mo →  ₹4,399/mo   +100%
```

18% GST checkout par. Advertised monthly figure ek division hai, billing cycle
nahi — poora term ek upfront invoice hai. Renewal par koi coupon ya referral
code kabhi nahi lagta.

Table me nahi hai to likhna nahi.

## Har post par lagne wale rule

- **Ek post, ek link.** Do link har jagah promotion padha jaata hai.
- **Pehle jawab, link aakhir me.** Link last line hai, pehli nahi.
- **Jo baat nuksan karti hai wo bhi likhein.** Ki KVM 4 ladder ka sabse ghatiya
  jump hai. Ki VPS poochne wale zyadatar logon ko VPS chahiye hi nahi. Yahi
  cheez upvote laati hai, remove hone se bachati hai — aur jo phir bhi
  khareedta hai wo sach me buyer tha.
- **Pucha jaaye to turant disclose karein** ki referral se kamate hain. Pakde
  jaane par nahi.
- **Upvote kabhi na maangein.** HN aur Reddit par permanent ban.
