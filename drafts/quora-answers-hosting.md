ANSWER 1
Sawaal: "How much does Hostinger cloud hosting cost, including renewal pricing?"
====================================================================

Cloud Startup is ₹599/month on Hostinger's India site, down from a listed ₹1,699 — that is their 65% sale rate, and it applies only to a first purchase.

The number that matters more: it renews at ₹1,599/month. So your second term costs roughly 2.7 times the first.

In total-cost terms, the 48-month term is ₹28,752 upfront against a regular price of ₹81,552. The same four years at the renewal rate would be ₹76,752.

Two things that are not in the advertised price:

1. GST. Hostinger's own footer says "Prices are listed without GST" — add 18% to every figure above.
2. The billing. ₹599/month is the 48-month total divided by 48. It is charged as one upfront payment, not monthly.

No coupon or referral discount applies to a renewal, so the term length you choose at signup is effectively the whole discount.

ANSWER 2
Sawaal: "Which domain registrar has fair renewal pricing without big price increases later?"
====================================================================

The honest answer is that almost no registrar has "fair" renewal pricing in the sense you mean — nearly all of them discount year one and charge the real rate from year two. What differs is how big the gap is.

So rather than looking for a registrar with no increase, do this instead:

Before you buy, look up the renewal price for that exact extension, not the registrar in general. A .com and a .in can have completely different renewal behaviour at the same registrar.

Ignore the first-year price entirely when comparing. Compare renewal to renewal. The registrar with the cheapest first year is frequently not the cheapest over five years.

Check whether transfer-out is free and whether they lock you in. A registrar with a slightly higher renewal but free transfers is safer than a cheap one that makes leaving painful.

Watch for add-ons that renew separately — WHOIS privacy especially. Some registrars give it free, some bill it every year.

One practical shortcut: if you are going to build a website anyway, hosting plans usually bundle a domain free for the first year. That removes one bill from year one, but the domain still renews at its own rate in year two — so the same homework applies.

I compared the Indian registrars by renewal rate rather than first-year price here: https://mohitkoli.in/blog/best-domain-registrars-india-2026

ANSWER 3
Sawaal: "What is the cheapest way to host a website in India?"
(ya milta-julta koi bhi "cheapest hosting India" wala sawaal)
====================================================================

The cheapest advertised plan in India right now is around ₹69/month, but that number is misleading in three specific ways, and it is worth knowing them before you buy.

First, it is not monthly. That rate requires a 48-month term paid upfront — about ₹3,312 in one payment.

Second, GST is extra. Hosting bought in India attracts 18% GST, and most hosts list prices without it.

Third, and this is the big one: the entry plan usually has the worst renewal. That ₹69 plan renews at ₹289/month — roughly 4.2 times. Meanwhile a mid-tier plan at ₹149 renews at ₹449, which is about 3 times.

Work that through and something surprising happens: four years on the mid-tier plan can cost less than staying on the cheapest plan past its first term.

So the genuinely cheapest approach is not "buy the lowest number". It is:

Pick the longest term you are confident you will actually use, because the discount only applies to the first term.

Compare plans on renewal rate, not sticker price.

Budget the GST so the bill is not a surprise.

ANSWER 4
Sawaal: "Is a VPS worth it over shared hosting for a small website?"
====================================================================

For a normal blog or business site, usually not. Shared hosting handles it, and a VPS mostly adds work you did not need.

A VPS becomes worth it at a fairly specific point, and it is not about traffic volume as often as people assume. It is worth it when:

You need to install things the host does not offer — Node.js, Docker, a background worker, a specific database version. Shared hosting will not let you.

You need root access for configuration you actually intend to change.

You are running something that must not be affected by a neighbour on the same box.

It is not worth it when:

You just want "faster". A well-configured shared plan is often faster than a badly-configured VPS.

Nobody on your side knows Linux. An unmanaged VPS means OS updates, security patches and backups are your job, and they genuinely are a job.

On price in India: entry VPS plans start around ₹599/month against roughly ₹69 for entry shared — so about nine times the cost, before counting your own time. The renewal gap is gentler on VPS though: the entry VPS roughly multiplies by 1.7 at renewal, while entry shared multiplies by 4.2.

Those two multipliers come from a renewal-rate table I keep for the Indian market, shared and VPS side by side: https://mohitkoli.in/blog/hostinger-renewal-price-india-2026

ANSWER 5
Sawaal: "How much email storage do I need for my business?"
(ya "business email kitne ka hota hai" type koi sawaal)
====================================================================

Less than you think, almost always.

A plain text email is roughly 10-20 KB. That means 5 GB holds hundreds of thousands of them. What actually fills a mailbox is attachments, not messages.

So the useful question is not how many emails you get, it is how big they are:

Mostly text, the occasional PDF — expect 1-2 GB a year. A 5 GB plan is fine for years.

Invoices, images and documents moving daily — expect 4-8 GB a year. Go for 20 GB.

Design files, large attachments, and you never delete anything — 50 GB.

On pricing in India, custom-domain business email starts around ₹29/month and goes to about ₹79/month for the 50 GB tier. Two things to watch:

It is priced per mailbox. A team of four is four times the cost, not one flat fee. This is the detail most comparisons skip.

The renewal roughly doubles — the ₹29 tier renews near ₹69.

One shortcut worth knowing: if you are buying web hosting anyway, mailboxes usually come included with the plan. Buying a separate email plan on top is then paying twice for the same thing.
