Everyone compares hosting on the sticker price. ₹69 a month, ₹149 a month, done.

Nobody compares the renewal multiplier — how many times your bill multiplies when the first term ends. So I worked it out for all 16 Hostinger India plans, and the ranking is almost exactly upside down from what the pricing page suggests.

Here's the headline: the cheapest plan in the catalogue has the worst renewal in the catalogue. The most expensive has the best.

Read that last line again

The ₹69 plan — the one on every billboard, in every "cheapest hosting in India" listicle — multiplies by 4.2x at renewal.

Here's what that looks like in rupees. Single costs ₹3,312 for its first 48 months. The next 48 months cost ₹13,872.

And here is the line that should change how you shop:

Premium's first term (₹7,152) is cheaper than Single's second term (₹13,872).

You can buy the better plan for four years and still pay less than staying on the cheap one. That is not a conclusion you reach by comparing ₹69 against ₹149.


Why the expensive plans renew more gently

It's not generosity. It's maths.

The renewal multiplier is a measure of how deep the introductory discount was, not how greedy the renewal is. Single is advertised at 83% off. You cannot discount something by 83% and have a soft landing — the gap is the multiplier.

Agency Startup is advertised at 50% off. Smaller gap, smaller landing.

So the rule generalises beyond Hostinger: the louder the discount percentage, the harder the renewal. Any host, any product. Check it yourself next time a "90% OFF" banner catches your eye.


Three practical conclusions

1. Term length is the entire discount.

The intro rate applies to your first term only, and no coupon or referral code applies to a renewal. So picking a 12-month term to "try it out" means you hit the renewal rate three years earlier than someone who picked 48 months. The term you choose at checkout matters more than which plan you choose.

2. Two things aren't in the price you see.

Hostinger's own footer says "Prices are listed without GST" — add 18%. And "₹69/month" is not billed monthly; it's the 48-month total divided by 48, charged upfront in one payment. ₹69/month means ₹3,312 leaving your account today.
